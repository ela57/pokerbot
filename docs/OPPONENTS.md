# Opponent strategies and attribution

These are practice heuristics, not claims of optimal play. No third-party code, preflop table, or thresholds are copied. Every YAML setting below is **OUR CHOSEN** parameterization. Ranges refer to percentile classes from this repository's generated table.

## SOURCED concepts

- [PokerStars: Other Poker Playing Styles](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/other-poker-playing-styles/) presents style as combinations of tight/loose and aggressive/passive.
- [PokerStars: Dealing with aggression](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/strategies/dealing-with-aggression-strategies-to-counter-hyper-aggressive-players/) distinguishes different aggressive player tendencies and discusses adapting to them.
- [PokerStars: Pot odds](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/lesson/pot-odds/) compares the price of a call with the equity needed to continue.
- [PokerStars: Push or fold](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/learn/strategies/push-or-fold/) covers short-stack preflop pressure and discusses 12 BB or less.

## OUR CHOSEN parameters and behavior

All styles have frozen parameter dataclasses in `pokerpractice/bots/opponents/strategies.py`; every decision uses the supplied seeded `random.Random`, position-aware ranges, legal action bounds, and only the observation. `weak` scales entry ranges up 15%, reduces aggression by 20%, and lowers strength thresholds; `strong` scales ranges down 10%, increases aggression by 15%, and raises strength thresholds. `normal` applies YAML values unchanged.

| Style | Chosen defaults / behavior |
|---|---|
| Nit | 8% open, 12% continue, 3% re-raise ranges; strong made-hand/value thresholds; 0.55-pot sizing. |
| TAG | 22% open, 30% continue, 9% re-raise; moderate aggression and 0.65-pot sizing. |
| LAG | 39% open, 49% continue, 17% re-raise; higher bluff/aggression frequencies and 0.82-pot sizing. |
| Calling station | 57% entry range, 67% continue range; passive checks/calls, rare raises only with strong hands. |
| Maniac | 76% open, 88% continue, 40% re-raise; 0.94 aggression frequency and 1.35-pot overbet sizing. |
| Equity/pot-odds | Preflop table plus postflop Monte Carlo against assumed ranges: prior preflop raise uses a 16% range, call a 48% range, otherwise uniform random. Continue when estimated equity clears pot odds plus a 0.025 margin. |
| Short-stack | At 15 BB or less, position-aware push/call thresholds (25% push, 14% call-push) with a chosen bubble-like payout-jump tightening heuristic; deeper play uses TAG-like ranges. This is not Nash or exact in-hand ICM. |

The equity workload is capped at 150 samples; the default is 30 to keep decision latency bounded. Measure competition-mode p99/max with `profile`; reduce the default if p99 regularly exceeds 1 ms. Preflop ranks are generated by `tools/build_preflop_table.py` using eval7 vs one uniform random hand, seed `20260217`, 1,000 deals per class (169 classes; 169,000 deals total). The output records its seed and sample count in `data/preflop_rank.json`.

The default roster has one of each seven styles. Duplicate styles are allowed, including seven copies of one style, and parameters/presets can be overridden per opponent in `configs/roster.yaml`.
